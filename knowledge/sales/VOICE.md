# VOICE — Project Alpha Sales Arabic

## Register
Respectful Masri. Not MSA, not street. حضرتك form.
How a competent Cairo sales professional talks to a business owner.

## T1 — Cold opener (VALIDATED by Adham, 25 Aug)

```
مساء الخير 🙏

شفت إن حضرتك عندكم {reviews} تقييم على جوجل بمتوسط {rating} — ده رقم محترم جداً.
بس مفيش موقع لـ {business_name}.

ممكن أعرف ليه مش عاملين موقع لحد دلوقتي؟
```

Structure: observation → gap → hook question. Never reorder.

## Corrections log

**#1 (Adham, 25 Aug):** عندك → **عندكم**
Reason: we are addressing a BUSINESS, not an individual. Plural is correct.
Knock-on: مش عامل → **مش عاملين** for agreement.

**Rule derived:** always address businesses in plural. عندكم / عاملين / بتشتغلوا / موقعكم.
Singular عندك is for a one-man operation only.

## Hard rules
- Real numbers only, from the scraped record. Never invent a review count.
- No rating/review data = do not send T1.
- Under 60 words.
- One question, always last.
- No price in a first message.
- Business name exactly as it appears on their listing.

## Banned
- MSA: نتشرف / يسعدنا أن / نود التواصل
- Generic: أهلاً وسهلاً بحضرتك as an opener
- Invented compliments
- Emoji spam (one maximum)
